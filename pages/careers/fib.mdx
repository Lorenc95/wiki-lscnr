# FIB

Federal duty. Same cop toolkit as [Police](/careers/police) — arrests, fines, dispatch, PD cars — with a **darker navy** name and blip, tactical kit, and a **separate gun bag**.

Needs the **police exam** (same one) and **Cop Level 20**. Cop Level is the XP track from police work (arrests, takedowns, seizures), not your criminal level.

Clock in at a **Police Station**, same desk as PD. Pick **Clock in as FIB** / **Switch job to FIB**.

<Callout type="info">
Same [Police Rules](/rules/police). Same slot cap. Same “no crime, no crew” while on duty.
</Callout>

## vs Police

| | Police | FIB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlock | Criminal **level 15** + exam | **Cop Level 20** + exam |
| Look | Blue | Dark navy |
| Guns | Police bag / PD Ammu | **FIB bag** / federal catalog |
| Stations & fleet | Shared | Shared |
| Arrests, dispatch, paycheck | Same | Same |

You're still **LEO**. Aiming a gun at you is **5 stars**. Wanted players who see your blip may shoot first. You still can't help suspects or your crew.

Clock back to Police or civilian at the same station.

## Tips

- **Do PD first.** Cop Level 20 is the gate — there's no FIB exam of its own.
- **Buy FIB guns on this job.** They don't carry over from your cop bag or your civ bag.
- **IAA** is a later undercover track. It's [not joinable right now](/careers/iaa).
