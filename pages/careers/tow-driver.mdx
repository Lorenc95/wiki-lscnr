# Tow Driver

Neutral **civilian** job. Rent a flatbed, pick up abandoned and player cars, drop them at the Davis impound. You are **not** a cop.

Needs **level 5**. Can't be wanted or have a bounty.

<Callout type="info">
Tow drivers are their own side for [teaming](/rules/ingame#2-teaming), but you still play by **civilian** combat rules. People may shoot you if you start towing **their** car — that's initiation.
</Callout>

## How to start

1. Go to the **Impound & Tow Depot** blip in **Davis** (the public lot).
2. Press **E** on the clerk. Clock in as **Tow Operator**.
3. Rent a **flatbed** (**$15,000** deposit from your bank — back when you return it).
4. Load a car, drive it to the same lot, drop it off.

Clock out at the same clerk. Return the truck if you want the deposit.

## The run

Dispatch can GPS you to a stranded car. You can also grab abandoned vehicles yourself — empty and sitting still long enough.

Park the flatbed close, get out, load it (short progress bar), then dump it at the depot.

| What you towed | Typical pay |
| --- | --- |
| Player-owned car | About **$20,000–$30,000** |
| Dispatch / abandoned | About **$5,000–$15,000** |
| AI traffic | About **$3,000–$5,000** |

No duty paycheck. The tows are the job.

Owners pick their car up at the same lot and pay a release fee. That's their problem, not yours.

## Rules

- You're a **civilian**. Don't help cops make arrests, and don't run with a crew as a "tow gunner."
- **Don't tow a car that's in a fight** just to grief. Towed player's car = they can shoot you.
- Hits on cops / EMS / tow aren't for you to take. See [Rule 1](/rules/ingame#1-rdm).
- You **can** still do civilian stuff. This job does **not** block crime the way Police does — but mixing a marked tow truck with a robbery is a bad look and a rules risk.

## Tips

- **Stay near Davis** if you want fast turn-ins. Long hauls eat the payday.
- **Return the flatbed.** Leave it and you burn the deposit.
- **Wait for dispatch** if the lot looks empty — calls fill in between player cars.
- Want law enforcement instead? That's [Police](/careers/police) at level 15.
