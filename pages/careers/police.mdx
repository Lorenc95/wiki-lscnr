# Police

Clock in at a **Police Station**, pass the exam, and play the cop side. Arrests, fines, dispatch, patrol cars. Your crew **does not count** while you're on duty.

Needs **criminal level 15** and a **passed exam**. Can't be wanted or have a bounty. There's a **slot cap** — if too many people are already cops, wait.

<Callout type="warning">
On duty you follow the [Police Rules](/rules/police). The main [Server Rules](/rules/ingame) still apply. Helping suspects or your crew is a ban.
</Callout>

## How to join

1. Hit **level 15**.
2. Go to any **Police Station** blip (Mission Row, La Mesa, Davis, Vespucci, Rockford, Vinewood, Del Perro, Sandy, Paleto).
3. Talk to the desk cop. If you haven't passed the exam, that's the first step — **$25,000** a try, **10 minutes** if you fail. Pass once and you're done forever.
4. Clock in as **Police**.

Clock out the same way, or switch to [FIB](/careers/fib) if your **Cop Level** is high enough.

You can't do [crime](/crime/small-time-robbery) on this job. Duty guns live in a **police bag** — civilian inventory stays on civ.

## On duty

Blue name and blip. Other cops don't take friendly fire from you.

**Y** next to a player is the police menu: fine, cuff, search, order them to stop.

| Wanted | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **1–2 stars** | Fine. No cuffs. |
| **3+ stars** | Cuff, search, jail. |
| **5+ / Most Wanted** | Kill on sight. Still arrest if they give up. |

Drive a cuffed suspect to a **station drop-off** (GPS when they're in your car) to book them. Low wanted is usually a ticket; high wanted is jail.

You'll get **dispatch** for robberies, murders, and similar. Don't camp [drug spots](/rules/police#6-camping-drug-spots-and-black-markets) waiting for a pinch — respond, then leave.

Patrol cars are at the **LSPD Vehicle Depot** (Mission Row lot). Buy them on this job; they go in the PD garage.

### Pay

A small **duty paycheck** hits your **bank** every **15 minutes** while you're on. Real money is **arrests, fines, and seizures** — higher wanted pays more. Searching someone only shows illegal items if they're high enough level.

## Combat

Wanted players who see your blip may **shoot first**. That's on you for showing up — it does **not** let you open fire first. [Police Rules §4](/rules/police#4-when-you-can-shoot).

Aiming a gun at a cop is **5 stars** for them. Hits on duty **don't** give you a kill reason.

Stun for non-threat arrests. Don't farm tires on a runner who hasn't shot and isn't red.

## Tips

- **Read the exam.** It's the [Police Rules](/rules/police) in quiz form.
- **Don't clock in for your crew.** You're not in it while the badge is on.
- **FIB** is the same toolkit with a darker name and a higher Cop Level gate. Same stations.
- **No slot?** That's the population cap. Play civ until someone clocks out.
