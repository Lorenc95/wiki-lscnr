# Getting Started

Welcome to Los Santos Cops and Robbers. You've just spawned at Los Santos International with a bit of cash and no idea what to do. This page covers the one rule that gets new players banned fastest, and everything the server actually lets you do.

<Callout type="warning">
**Read this first:** [Server Rules](/rules/ingame). This page is a tour, not a rulebook. If the two ever disagree, the rules page wins.
</Callout>

## This is not a deathmatch server

LSCnR is **Cops and Robbers**, not free-for-all PvP. You cannot walk up to a random player and shoot them because they were standing there.

Killing a player without a valid reason is **RDM** (random deathmatch), and it is punished — warnings, then bans, and repeat offenders lose their progress.

You need a reason to attack someone. The full list is in [Rule 1](/rules/ingame#1-rdm), but the short version:

| You can fight them when | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **They started it** | They shot at you, aimed at you, rammed you, or threatened you. See [Initiation](/rules/ingame#1b-initiation). |
| **They did it to you already** | They killed, robbed, or arrested you and the revenge icon is showing. One kill back. See [Revenge](/rules/ingame#1c-revenge). |
| **They're wanted at 5+ stars** | Red name, or the dark red Most Wanted. Kill on sight for everyone but EMS. |
| **There's a hit on them** | The hit amount shows behind their name. |
| **You're both in a PvP zone** | [Redzones, active airdrops](/features/redzones-and-airdrops), and any robbery in progress. |
| **You're wanted and a cop shows up** | See [Criminal Rules](/rules/criminals). |

<Callout type="error">
**Not a reason to kill someone:** they drove past you, they looked at you, they're being chased by police, or you just felt like it. That's RDM.
</Callout>

If someone kills you without a reason, don't retaliate outside the rules — clip it and open a ticket on **Discord**.

## Your first 15 minutes

1. **Get some starting cash.** [Parking meters](/crime/small-time-robbery#parking-meters) or a legal job will get you moving without needing a weapon.
2. **Grab a phone.** Buy one at **DigitalDen**. It's your hub for [quick jobs](/features/phone), [hits](/features/bounty-and-hits), insurance, the [stock market](/features/stocks), and more.
3. **Buy gear.** Most early activities need one specific item — see the table below.
4. **Pick a lane.** Crime pays faster and gets you wanted. Legal work is slower and safe. You can do both.

### Gear worth buying early

| Item | Where | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Phone**](/features/phone) | [DigitalDen](/features/shops) | Quick jobs, hits, insurance, stocks |
| **Lockpicks** | [Hardware store, or the black market dealer](/features/shops) | [Parking meters](/crime/small-time-robbery#parking-meters) |
| **Fishing rod** | [Hardware store](/features/shops) | [Fishing](/jobs/fishing) |
| **A weapon** | [Ammu-Nation](/features/shops) | [Small stores](/crime/small-time-robbery#small-stores) and [nightclub](/crime/nightclub-robbery) hold-ups |

<Callout type="info">
**Buy the gun before you plan the robbery.** Store, nightclub, and gun store holdups all need a weapon in hand. Walking in empty-handed just wastes the trip.
</Callout>

## Crime

Start small, work up. The bigger the score, the more cops and players it pulls.

### On your own

| Job | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| [**Parking meters**](/crime/small-time-robbery#parking-meters) | Lockpicks |
| [**Vending machines**](/crime/small-time-robbery#vending-machines) | A crowbar from Ammu-Nation. It unlocks at a level, so this one waits a bit. |
| [**Small stores**](/crime/small-time-robbery#small-stores) | A weapon. Rob the register solo; Ammu, clothing, barbers, and the rest use the same hold-up. |
| [**ATM hacks**](/crime/atm-hacking) | An ATM hacking USB from the black market (dumpsters can drop one). |
| [**Nightclubs**](/crime/nightclub-robbery) | A weapon |
| [**House robberies**](/crime/house-robberies) | Get in touch with **The Boss** to get started. |

### Better with a crew

| Job | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| [**Store safes**](/crime/small-time-robbery#safes) | The safe in the back needs **two people**. Bigger payout than the register. |
| [**Bank trucks**](/crime/bank-trucks) | C4. First to grab the cash gets the pot. |
| [**Vanilla Unicorn**](/crime/unicorn-heist) | Lockpick, hacking laptop, **3–5** people. |
| [**Banks**](/crime/bank-heists) | Bring a small crew. This is the big one. |

### Ongoing money

- **[Drugs](/crime/drugs)** — grow your own or cook them, then find buyers and sell.
- **[Car exports](/crime/car-exports)** — steal and deliver cars for extra cash. *(Boosting is coming soon.)*

## Police

It's Cops and Robbers — you can play the other side.

To join the [force](/careers/police) you need to be **level 15** and **pass the cop exam**. [FIB](/careers/fib) is the same toolkit at **Cop Level 20**. Once you're on duty, the [Police Rules](/rules/police) apply, and your crew means nothing while you're wearing the badge.

[Tow](/careers/tow-driver) is a separate civilian career at **level 5**. [EMS](/careers/ems), [IAA](/careers/iaa), [Army](/careers/army), and [Fire](/careers/fireman) aren't joinable right now.

## Things to do with other players

- **[Racing](/features/racing)** — set up and race against other players.
- **[Hits](/features/bounty-and-hits)** — put a contract on a player through the phone. It needs a **valid reason**, like initiation or revenge. A hit placed without one counts as RDM, and that's on you, not the person who takes it.
- **Quick jobs** — short jobs straight from your [phone](/features/phone).
- **[Airdrops](/features/redzones-and-airdrops)** — timed supply drops. PvP is on inside the zone while it's active.
