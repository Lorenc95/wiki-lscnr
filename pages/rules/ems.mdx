# EMS Rules

<Callout type="info">
This page covers the rules for playing as a paramedic. The main [Server Rules](/rules/ingame) still apply to you as well. For when criminals may fight, see [Criminal Rules](/rules/criminals). Cop rules are on [Police Rules](/rules/police).
</Callout>

## The short version

| Rule | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Stay neutral** | Do not side with criminals or cops. You may take priority over reviving a cop before a criminal, but it is not mandatory. |
| **Medical aid only on the spot** | Revive and heal through the script. You are not a getaway driver or a gunman. |
| **Force is for the scene** | Shoot only to protect yourself or during a revive you are actively performing. Stop when the threat is over. Do not join a shootout to kill participants; wait until the fight is over. |
| **Wanted level is not your target** | A red name, Most Wanted status, or a hit is not a reason for you to kill someone. |
| **Redzones are not kill on sight** | Approaching or entering a redzone or airdrop does not make you a target. |
| **Only a criminal under pressure can wave you off** | They may tell you not to pick up the person they are fighting. If you revive that person anyway, they may kill you. |
| **No abusing EMS for hostages** | You cannot deliberately use a downed player or an EMS call to lure a paramedic into a hostage situation. A clip showing the arrival and interaction is required for reports. |
| **Cops may not kill you** | A police officer may not kill EMS in any case. Abuse is a report, not a shoot. |

---

## 1. Stay neutral

EMS is its own side. Criminals are the other side. You do not join them.

- Don't team up with cops or with criminals.
- Don't fight for either side, or hold a position so one side can win. You are there to revive, not to participate in combat.
- Don't be a private medic for a crew, a gang, or one player.
- Don't ride with cops or ride with criminals.
- Don't drive cops or criminals away from their enemies.
- Don't tell one side where the other side is.
- Don't refuse to treat one side because you prefer the other.
- Only help suspects through the scripted revive and heal.

## 2. When EMS can be killed

On duty, you are not a free target. These are the only reasons a **criminal or civilian** may kill you.

- You are reviving or healing their enemy after they told you not to do so, or it is obvious that you are doing so.
- You are staying close enough to their enemy to revive or heal them.
- You move within assault-rifle range while an enemy nearby could still be revived.
- You initiate on them. Aiming, shooting, an intentional ram, a kill threat, or joining a side's fight all count.
- They have revenge on you, and the revenge icon is showing.

<Callout type="warning">
**A police officer may not kill EMS in any case.** Reviving a suspect, shooting, a red name, Most Wanted status, a hit, or a redzone does not give a cop a reason to shoot you. If an officer believes you are abusing the job, they report you. They do not kill you.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**PVP zones do not make EMS kill on sight.** Redzones and airdrops do not apply to you. Approaching one, crossing the edge, standing in one, or going in to treat someone is not a reason to shoot you. The zone is not initiation.
</Callout>

Nobody may kill you for:

- Being on duty, driving to a call, or standing by.
- Approaching, entering, or standing in a redzone or airdrop.
- Reviving their friend or someone on their own side.
- Looking at them, driving past, or being near a chase without helping the other side.
- Treating downed players after the fight is clearly over.

Taking you hostage to make you work for someone is not allowed. Killing you because you would not obey is not allowed, except for the rule in the next section.

They may force a gun on you, telling you to revive their side before police, but this only applies to civilians.

Do not follow the police to a fight. Do not trail a cop car, stage outside a scene they are heading to, or show up with them so you can revive their side.

Arriving because you were called to a downed player, or crossing a fight that was already happening, can be an accident. Once or twice is not punishment.

<Callout type="warning">
**Doing it on purpose, or doing it over and over, is teaming.** Staff can ban you from the EMS job. That is not a server ban. You lose the paramedic job for using it as police backup.
</Callout>

## 3. Telling EMS not to pick someone up

Only a **criminal holding you under pressure** may tell you not to revive someone.

Under pressure means they are in that fight right now. They are being shot at, they are shooting, or the person on the ground will be a direct threat to them the moment that person gets up.

- They may tell you clearly not to pick that person up.
- You stop that revive while the pressure is still real.
- A criminal who is safe, watching from a distance, already out of the fight, or calling it in for a friend may not give this order.
- A cop may not give this order. Another paramedic may not give it.
- When the pressure ends, the order ends. If that criminal dies, runs, or the fight is clearly over, you may revive the person you were told to leave.

<Callout type="warning">
**If you revive them anyway, that criminal may kill you.** Ignoring the order is a reason for the criminal who gave it to shoot you. This does not let them kidnap you, force you to revive their friends, or shoot you for treating someone who is not their enemy.
</Callout>

## 4. Abusing EMS to take hostages

**You may not call EMS to a downed player and then deliberately refuse to revive them to use the paramedic as a hostage.** EMS responding to a call does not give you the right to hold them hostage simply because they arrived at the scene.

- Do not call or lure EMS to a downed player with the intention of holding the paramedic hostage instead of having a legitimate medical interaction.
- Do not abuse the revive mechanic to force a paramedic into a hostage situation.
- If you intend to take EMS hostage, you must follow the applicable server rules for hostage-taking. An EMS call or a downed player does not automatically make the paramedic a valid hostage.

<Callout type="warning">
**A clip is required for reports of this type.** The clip must show EMS arriving at the scene and the subsequent interaction, including the alleged hostage-taking. The recording must start early enough to establish the context of the call and what happened after arrival. Clips that begin after EMS has already arrived may not provide sufficient context to establish what happened.
</Callout>

**The following situations do not automatically count as holding EMS hostage:**

- A criminal tells EMS not to revive their enemy while the criminal is actively under pressure, in accordance with Section 3.
- EMS is asked to revive someone, but the criminal revives another player first or deals with the situation before EMS can complete the revive.
- EMS arrives at an active scene, and the situation changes before they can provide medical assistance.
- A player simply delays or declines medical assistance without using the situation to hold EMS hostage.

**Reviving someone else first, delaying a revive, or not immediately accepting EMS assistance does not automatically constitute hostage-taking.**

Staff will assess the full interaction and available evidence before taking action.

## 5. Use of force

You may shoot only to:

- Defend yourself.
- Stop someone who is an immediate threat while you are reviving or healing.

Retreat when you reasonably can. Stop shooting as soon as the threat is over. Don't chase. Don't finish a suspect for the police, and don't finish an enemy for a criminal.

Red name, Most Wanted status, and hit targets are not your targets. Wanted level is never your reason to shoot.

## 6. Other restrictions

- Don't carjack innocent civilians, cops, or other paramedics.
- Don't damage unattended player vehicles on purpose.
- Don't use a trunk, a kidnapping, or any off-script revive to get around these rules.
