# Rules

By playing on LSCnR, you agree to these rules. Not knowing the rules, or being a new player, does not exempt you from them. ## At a glance

**Combat & PvP**
- **No RDM** — do not attack or kill players without a valid reason: red-name (5-star wanted level), Most Wanted, initiation, redzones, airdrop zones, active robbery scenes, or the EMS engagement rules.
- **EMS are neutral** and may only use force where these rules explicitly allow it.
- **No emoting** during fights or chases.
- **No combat logging**, except when an EMS player is unlawfully attempting to kill you.
- **No teaming** across sides.
- **No kill feeding.**

**Fair Play**
- **No cheating or exploits** — including bugs, unintended mechanics, and third-party tools.

**Community Conduct**
- **No discrimination, threats, hate speech, scams, or impersonation.**
- **English only.**
- **Minimum age: 16+.**
- **No report spamming.**
- **No begging** staff for items, money, VIP, unbans, or favors.
- **Persistent negative behavior** may result in permanent removal.

<Callout type="warning">
Staff may make exceptions with management approval. **The summary above does not replace the full rules below.**
</Callout>

## 1. RDM

Attacking or killing another player without a valid reason, defined here as RDM, is not allowed. You may not attack or kill any player except when permitted by these rules.

<details>
<summary>**a. Valid kill reasons**</summary>

No additional justification is needed beyond the situation itself:

**Red-name bounty (5-star wanted level)**
Any player with a 5-star wanted level and a red name is kill-on-sight for all players. No initiation is required from either side.

**Most Wanted**
The Most Wanted player is the single player currently holding the highest active bounty on the server. Reaching this status automatically elevates a player to a **6-star wanted level**, with their name and blip turning **dark red**. Only one player holds this status at a time, and they are KOS for all players — no initiation required.

<Callout type="info">
If you are the Most Wanted player, you have the same self-defense rights as any other player, plus the right to pre-fire any player visibly approaching you with clear hostile intent. A player who happens to be nearby without showing hostile intent is **not** a valid target.
</Callout>

Players who don't want to be engaged should keep their distance from anyone marked with a dark red name and blip.

**Active hit contract**
If a hit contract has been placed on a player, that player is KOS for all players not currently working as a cop, paramedic, or tow driver — regardless of the hit amount.

Hit visibility is tiered:

- **Under 450,000** — the hit amount is shown on the target's screen and displayed behind their name in the world and on the scoreboard. There is no map blip; players can only identify the target on sight.
- **450,000 and above** — everything above applies, and additionally the target's name and blip turn **bright blue**, making them trackable on the map by the entire server.

<Callout type="warning">
You must have a valid reason (initiation, revenge, or another reason listed in these rules) to place a hit on someone. Placing a hit without a valid reason is treated as RDM, regardless of who carries it out.
</Callout>

If you have an active hit on you at any tier, you have the same self-defense rights as any other player, plus the right to pre-fire any player visibly approaching you with clear hostile intent. A player who happens to be nearby without showing hostile intent is not a valid target.

**Initiation**
A player initiates on you when they do any of the following:

- Aim or shoot a weapon at you
- Intentionally ram your vehicle
- Verbally threaten to kill you
- Verbally insult you or your crew (in VC or in chat) — have a clip of this ready to avoid false bans
- Move within assault rifle range of you with clear hostile intent (this does **not** apply if *you* move into *their* AR range)
- Work against you
- Keep following you around without a valid reason

Once a player initiates on you, you may retaliate. If the initiator is presumably together with other players (e.g. in the same vehicle or clearly grouped), you may engage those players as well.

**Initiation expires when:**

- The initiator dies
- You kill the initiator
- Both parties clearly agree the engagement has ended

After initiation expires you need a fresh valid reason to engage that player again.

**Redzones and the airdrop zone**
If you are inside a marked redzone or the active airdrop zone, you may engage any player occupying it. If a target leaves the redzone, you may not chase them out of it with the intent to kill unless you have a separate valid reason to do so.

**Active robbery scenes**
During any robbery in progress — store, bank, jewelry heist, or any other robbery job — all players physically inside the robbery location are valid targets. This applies whether or not police are online or responding. Criminals defending the robbery, and any players who enter the building or location, do so on their own terms. Bystanders in the surrounding area who are not inside the active location are not automatically valid targets, but if they enter the scene, they accept the risk.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**b. Revenge**</summary>

Being attacked, killed, arrested, or having your property taken or stolen gives you the right to kill the responsible player once within the same game session. This includes failed attempts. If you are attacked again, your right to revenge is reinstated.

This applies to tow drivers as well — beginning to tow your vehicle counts as initiation (see Rule 2d), which gives you the right to retaliate and satisfies the property-taken condition for revenge.

When both players kill each other simultaneously (a trade), revenge is not considered satisfied and both players may seek it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**c. Cops and criminals**</summary>

When you are a wanted suspect, you may kill police officers if they are:

- Chasing or attempting to pull you or your friends over
- Shooting at you or your friends
- Arresting one of your friends
- Camping a robbery location for a sustained period
- Moving toward your position and getting within assault rifle range, except when you are near a police spawn location
- Close enough that their police blip becomes visible to you while you are wanted — this counts as initiation by the officer

Once any of these conditions are met, initiation has been established. You may continue pursuing and engaging that officer even if they attempt to disengage or flee — fleeing does not cancel initiation.

See [Rule 18 (Cop Hunting)](#18-cop-hunting) for how these rules apply to unwanted players engaging cops.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**d. Civilians and EMS**</summary>

When playing as a civilian or criminal, you may attack or kill a paramedic if they are:

- Reviving an enemy or opponent
- Healing an enemy or opponent
- Remaining close enough to enemies that they are able to revive or heal them
- Moving within assault rifle range while nearby enemies could be revived

Additional rules:

- You may intimidate EMS to stop reviving enemies.
- You may **not** take EMS hostage to force them to work for you.
- You may **not** kill EMS simply because they revived your friend.
- Randomly attacking EMS is RDM.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**e. What is RDM**</summary>

- Shooting a player with no bounty, no initiation, no redzone, and no active robbery scene
- Killing someone because they "looked at you" or drove near you without any of the triggers above
- Killing someone after the valid reason has expired (e.g. after the fight ended, after they left the redzone, after the robbery concluded)

</details>

## 2. Cross-Teaming

Working with or assisting the opposing side to gain an advantage is not allowed. The sides on LSCnR are: **criminals, cops, paramedics, and tow drivers**. Each section below specifies what teaming means for that role.

<details>
<summary>**a. Civilian / Criminal**</summary>

- May not team up with or assist cops or paramedics
- May not help them in a fight unless you have your own independent valid reason to engage their target
- May not drive them away from their opponents
- May not be in a vehicle with a cop while they are performing police duties or while you are committing crimes
- May not team with EMS
- May not help EMS in fights unless another rule independently allows it
- May not help EMS escape from enemies by driving them away

</details>

<details>
<summary>**b. Cop**</summary>

- May not team up with or assist suspects, except through the scripted bribe feature
- May not drive a wanted suspect anywhere unless they are in your custody being transported to a police station — no detours, no favors
- May not help a suspect in a fight unless you have your own independent valid reason to engage their target
- Has a duty to protect life — may not watch a suspect attack a civilian or cuffed player and do nothing
- May not watch suspects attack other cops or paramedics and do nothing
- If transporting a passenger who is not a cop, paramedic, or suspect in custody, may not engage in police activity until they leave the vehicle
- If your passenger commits a crime, you are expected to stop the vehicle and detain them
- Must not prevent another cop from completing an arrest or attempt to search their cuffed suspect without permission

</details>

<details>
<summary>**c. Paramedic**</summary>

Paramedics are neutral emergency responders whose primary duty is providing medical aid.

**Cross-teaming**

- May not team up with or assist suspects except through the scripted revive and heal mechanics
- May not become a private medic for any gang or player
- May not help suspects during fights unless another rule independently permits it
- May not drive suspects away from enemies or police
- If transporting a suspect and police or enemies arrive, must stop and allow the situation to play out

**Use of force**

<Callout type="warning">
EMS may only use lethal force when:
- Acting in self-defense
- Defending another paramedic
- Stopping criminals posing an immediate threat while reviving or healing

EMS has a duty to retreat whenever reasonably possible, and must immediately stop shooting once the threat ends.
</Callout>

**Carjacking** — EMS may not carjack innocent civilians, police officers, or other paramedics.

**Property damage** — EMS may not intentionally damage unattended player vehicles.

**Most Wanted** — Most Wanted (6-star) and Red-name (5-star) players may be killed by everyone *except* EMS. EMS may not execute players solely because they are Most Wanted or Red-name.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**d. Tow driver**</summary>

Tow drivers are neutral civilians. Standard civilian rules apply — no additional teaming restrictions apply to tow drivers beyond those that apply to all civilians.

Beginning to tow another player's vehicle counts as initiation against that vehicle's owner. The owner may retaliate under the normal initiation rules.

</details>

## 3. Cheating

<Callout type="error">
Using any modification, external program, or third-party tool that gives you an unfair advantage is not allowed. **Don't cheat or mod — you will be banned and your progress may be reset.**
</Callout>

| Category | Not Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| **Gameplay-altering tools** | Mod menus, wallhacks, noclip (of any sort), health lock, damage modifiers, recoil scripts, rapid fire tools, triggerbots, aim assistance, cheat menus, softaim, etc. |
| **Visual/world mods** | Ragdoll physics changes, object removal, foliage/prop removal (leaves, bushes, water, electrical cabinets, phone booths, etc.), collision removal |
| **Network abuse** | VPN-induced lag, lag switching, similar network manipulation |
| **Player conduct** | Knowingly playing with a cheater without attempting to leave or reporting them |
| **Weapon mods** | RPF mods that change weapon behavior in any way |
| **Texture mods** | Anything that makes camouflage, ghillie suits, or hidden players easier to spot |
| **HUD/minimap mods** | Extended radar range, player blips beyond normal detection radius |
| **Vehicle/physics exploits** | Teleport hacks, infinite handbrake/traction glitches used to cheese chases |
| **General rule** | If it's not meant to be used and gives you a benefit over other players — beyond visual mods — it's not allowed unless explicitly listed below |

### Exceptions / Allowed mods

| Allowed | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **No-advantage mods** | Any modification that gives no advantage over other players |
| **Static crosshair overlay** | A simple static crosshair that does not read game memory, automate inputs, or provide any advantage beyond a visual overlay. If it does more, treat it as **not allowed** unless staff confirm otherwise |
| **Sound/visual mods** | Sound packs, graphic mods, Reshade, and FOV changes |
| **Simple macros** | Macros that send text, commands, or hotkeys only |
| **Graphics enhancement packs** | Fine as long as they don't remove objects, water, etc. |
| **Skydome** | Custom skydomes are completely fine and allowed |
| **Tracers** | Custom tracers are allowed |
| **Weather mods** | Fine as long as they don't provide an unfair advantage |
| **No shadows** | Allowed — improves FPS only, no real advantage |

Staff may determine whether a specific modification is allowed on a case-by-case basis. If staff deem a mod not allowed that isn't explicitly listed above, you may still be banned for it.

## 4. Exploiting

<Callout type="error">
Exploiting any bug, glitch, script error, or unintended mechanic of LSCnR or GTA V to gain an advantage is not allowed. **Don't exploit — you will be banned and your progress may be reset.**
</Callout>

Some examples of prohibited exploits include:

- Using emotes mid-combat to avoid a negative outcome or clip through geometry
- Killing players who are on the revive screen, still loading in, or otherwise unable to control their character
- Intentionally forcing a wanted level onto another player unfairly
- Ghost peeking or other GTA V physics exploits used to gain a combat advantage
- Spelling banned words differently to bypass the chat filter
- Abusing money, XP, or item duplication loops

This list is not exhaustive. Staff may ban for exploiting on a case-by-case basis.

<Callout type="info">
If you find an exploit, report it in a private support ticket. You are **not** required to report it, but sharing it with others or encouraging its abuse is a bannable offense.
</Callout>

## 5. Combat Logging

Disconnecting from the server to avoid being killed, arrested, or otherwise penalized is not allowed.

You are considered in combat if you were involved in a fight or active police chase within the last **3 minutes**. The minimap is not the sole indicator — if you were being chased and have not clearly escaped or resolved the situation, assume you are still in combat.

Examples of combat logging include disconnecting mid-fight or mid-chase.

<Callout type="info">
**Overdosing is an intended gameplay mechanic and is never considered combat logging.** Players are free to use drugs at their own discretion, even if doing so results in an overdose.
</Callout>

### Exception

Combat logging is permitted if an EMS player is unlawfully attempting to kill you in violation of these rules.

### Accidental disconnects

If you disconnect accidentally while in combat, reconnect within **5 minutes** and return to the situation when reasonable. Failing to reconnect, or reconnecting and not returning, may be treated as combat logging.

## 10. Emotes

Using emotes during a fight or an active chase is not allowed. No exceptions.

Using emotes during events is not allowed unless staff explicitly permit it for that specific event.

## 11. Ghost Peeking

Abusing wall or cover geometry so that your bullets clip through solid surfaces in ways the engine was not intended to allow is not allowed.

### What is *not* ghost peeking

Some vehicles have asymmetric collision — for example, a car where a player can shoot out the back window but bullets cannot pass through that same window going in. This is a known quirk of GTA V vehicle collision and is **not** a punishable offense. Reports of this nature will be denied. Adjust your gameplay by flanking, repositioning, shooting the tires, or forcing the player out of the vehicle.

Ghost peeking applies specifically to abusing wall and cover geometry on foot, not to vehicle collision quirks.

## 12. Safe Zones

Marked safe zones — hospitals, spawns, and other designated spots — are no-combat areas, shown in-game as large green circles on the map with an on-screen indicator confirming when you are inside one. If you do not see the indicator, you are not protected.

<details>
<summary>**Entry delay**</summary>

<Callout type="warning">
Safe zone protection does not apply instantly. There is a **~10 second delay** after entering a safe zone before protection activates. You cannot sprint into a safe zone mid-fight and expect immediate protection — players engaging you may legally continue to do so during that window.
</Callout>

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Wanted players and hit targets**</summary>

Safe zones do not protect players with an active wanted level or an active hit contract. Cops may engage and arrest wanted players inside a safe zone. Any player with a hit on them remains a valid target inside a safe zone, consistent with how bounty and wanted players are treated. Other players may not RDM an unrelated player inside a safe zone, but engaging a wanted or hit player is not RDM.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Not allowed inside safe zones**</summary>

- Killing or attacking players
- Baiting fights or provoking players to drag them out
- Camping to farm easy kills

Using a safe zone to escape a fight you started or were losing is abuse of mechanics and may be treated as combat logging or exploiting.

</details>

## 15. Persistent Negative Behavior

Some players are not breaking a single specific rule but consistently make the server worse for everyone around them. That counts here.

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- **Constant chat spam** — flooding global or local chat, refusing to stop when asked
- **Constant harassment** — repeatedly targeting the same player in low-level ways that add up over time
- **Constant drama** — stirring conflict between players, staff, or communities every session
- **Draining behavior** — being the player who is always the reason a tense ticket exists, even when no single moment crosses a hard rule line

<Callout type="warning">
If your presence consistently makes the server worse for the people around you, staff may issue a permanent removal regardless of whether each individual incident crossed a hard rule line. This is about a pattern, not a bad day. Staff will consider history, ticket volume, and how other players react to your presence.
</Callout>

## 16. Bug Reports

If you find a bug, do not abuse it. Collect proof and report it in a private support ticket. Reporting a bug will never get you punished. Hiding one will.

Intentionally concealing a bug because it benefits you is treated as exploit abuse. This includes quietly farming a money or item duplication bug, sharing a bug privately with friends so your group can use it before staff find out, and continuing to use a known exploit instead of reporting it.

<Callout type="error">
**Consequence: ban and full account reset.** Any progress, items, or currency gained while concealing a bug will be wiped.
</Callout>

## 17. Minimum Age

You must be at least **16 years old** to play on LSCnR. If you are under 16, you may only remain on the server if staff have explicitly agreed that you have demonstrated clear maturity. That permission can be revoked at any time if your behavior does not hold up. Lying about your age or attempting to bypass this rule can be punished.

## 18. Cop Hunting

LSCnR does not have "cop hunting" as a separate mechanic. Engaging a police officer is governed entirely by the RDM rules in [Rule 1](#1-rdm), not by the fact that they are a cop.

- **No wanted level (0 stars):** You may not attack, shoot at, or attempt to kill a police officer, including by ramming them with a vehicle. This is RDM. Being a cop, being nearby, or performing police duties is not on its own a valid kill reason.
- **1 star or higher:** Once you have an active wanted level, an officer attempting to pull you over, chase you, or arrest you has initiated on you under Rule 1c (Cops and criminals). You may retaliate against that officer under the normal initiation rules.

Attacking cops with no wanted level is considered RDM.

## 19. Code of Conduct

<details>

<Callout type="error">

- Doxxing (sharing someone's name, address, phone number, or other identifying information)

English is the only permitted language in text and voice chat for the purposes of fair moderation and communication.

Advertising other servers, communities, products, or services without permission is not allowed.

</details>

The report system exists to address real rule breaks. Do not use it to pressure staff after losing a fight, mass-report someone over a personal dispute, refile the same complaint after staff have already responded, or flood tickets with reports that have no evidence. Reports opened for situations that are clearly valid kills under Rule 1 will be denied. Repeat offenders will be blacklisted from the report system and may be banned.

When submitting a report, include a clip and/or screenshots with enough context for staff to see what happened and when. Reports without evidence may not be actionable. Malicious or false reports can be punished.

</details>

Staff decisions, punishments, investigations, and internal actions are not public discussion topics.

If you disagree with a punishment or staff decision, use the appropriate appeal, report, or ticket process. Public discussion is not the place to challenge or debate staff actions.

<Callout type="warning">
Continuing to discuss staff decisions after being told to stop may result in further punishment.

</details>

## 20. Begging

Begging staff for items, money, VIP, unbans, or any other personal favor is not allowed.

- **Asking to become staff** will blacklist you from ever becoming staff.
- **Asking staff for money, VIP, unbans, or other personal favors** results in a mute or temporary suspension.

<Callout type="info">
Legitimate requests — such as a refund or compensation ticket for a genuine bug or server issue — are not begging and should go through the normal ticket process.
</Callout>
