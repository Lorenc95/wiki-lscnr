# BALLSAQ — Player Guide

**BALLSAQ** is Los Santos’s in-game stock exchange. You trade it from the **Stocks** app on your phone. This isn’t Wall Street homework it’s a living server market with its own personality, news, and crowd behaviour.

Companies on the board are the ones you already know from around the state: Merryweather, Ammu-Nation, eCola, Vapid, Maze Bank, Cluckin’ Bell, and the rest.

---

## Quick start

1. Open your **phone** → **Stocks**.
2. Browse the **Market** tab for listings, or **Mine** for what you already own.
3. Tap a company → pick **Buy** or **Sell** → choose how many shares → confirm.
4. Trades settle against your **bank** balance.

Prices do **not** twitch every second. The board updates on a schedule (a few times a day). You’ll see a countdown toward the next move while the app is open.

---

## How trading works

### Ask, bid, and fees

You never trade at the exact “middle” price on the board:

- **Ask** — what you pay when you **buy** (a little above the quoted price).
- **Bid** — what you get when you **sell** (a little below the quoted price).
- **Brokerage** — a small fee on buys *and* sells.

So going in and straight back out costs you money even if the price didn’t move. A stock has to move in your favour before you’re actually ahead. Treat that like the house edge at a casino table small per trade, painful if you spam it.

### What you’re looking at

- **Portfolio value** — what your shares would fetch if you sold them *right now* (at the bid, after the usual costs are considered in the app’s wording).
- **Profit / loss** — how that compares to what you paid in.
- **Chart** — recent price history for that company.
- **News** — headlines that can swing a stock hard when they hit.

## Market mood

At the top of the Stocks app you’ll see the overall market mood. This is a **server-wide** vibe, not one company:

| Mood | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Bullish** | The tape is leaning green. Wind at your back. |
| **Bearish** | The tape is leaning red. Headwinds. |
| **Flat** | No strong lean either way. |

Mood alone doesn’t make you rich or broke — individual stocks still do their own thing but it’s useful context for the next move.

## Company “heat” (the important labels)

Each listing also shows how the **crowd** is treating it right now. Read these. They’re there on purpose.

| Label | Short tag | Plain English |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Crowd piling in** | Overbought | Everybody’s buying. That’s usually when it gets punished. |
| **Getting busy** | Heating up | Interest is building. The longer that runs, the uglier the next move can get. |
| **Nobody's watching** | Ignored | Nobody owns it. Left alone, it tends to drift up — until someone buys in and kills that climb. |
| **Somebody owns it** | Held | Someone’s sitting on shares. The “ignored climb” isn’t in play. |
| **Crowd bailing out** | Selling off | People are dumping. That’s often when the fall starts to ease. |

**Rule of thumb the board keeps teaching you:** the crowd is usually wrong. If half the server is stampeding into the same ticker, you’re not early you’re the exit liquidity.

## What moves prices?

BALLSAQ is a **simulated Los Santos market**. It’s tied into the life of the city — industry, crime, panic, hype, quiet weeks, and the kind of chaos LS is famous for not a sterile spreadsheet.

In practice, the tape reacts to things like:

- **What players are doing on the exchange** — buying and selling *does* matter. Heavy crowd buying into one name is a classic setup for pain. Quiet names nobody wants can drift the other way.
- **Market mood** — bullish / bearish / flat pressure across the board.
- **Sector swings** — related companies (banks, telecoms, defense, food, etc.) often lean together.
- **News** — random (and dramatic) headlines. Good news and bad news both happen. Big prints are rare enough that they feel like events, not noise.
- **The wider city vibe** — robberies, businesses, crime waves, boom times, and general LS nonsense are part of the *fiction* of why the market exists. You’re trading a living GTA economy, not the NYSE. Don’t expect a neat “I robbed one store → MER up 3%” receipt. Expect a market that *feels* like San Andreas.
- **Ingame Actions** - All your ingame actions will reflect back on in on the stockmarket.

Prices only **settle** on the scheduled ticks. Between ticks, the board is waiting.

## News tab

When something big happens to a company, it can show up as a headline (and a sharp move). That’s the “get lucky / get wrecked” half of the game. You can’t force news. You can only decide whether you’re holding when it hits.

## Tips that actually help

1. **Don’t FOMO-chase Overbought names.** If the label says the crowd is piling in, the app is warning you.
2. **Respect fees.** Scalping in and out all day is how portfolios quietly die.
3. **Ignored ≠ free money forever.** The climb lasts while nobody owns it. The second the crowd notices, the story changes.
4. **Size matters.** One person poking around barely registers. A mob all buying the same ticker is how crashes get written.
5. **Diversify a little** if you’re playing with serious bank — one bad headline on your only holding hurts.
6. **Treat it like gambling with homework.** Some people finish ahead. The market is not a salary. Only risk what you can afford to watch go red.
7. **Hold through the countdown.** Staring at the clock doesn’t change the next tick — the heat labels and news matter more than refreshing every ten seconds.

## Common questions

### Does buying and selling affect the price?
**Yes.** Player trading is a real part of how BALLSAQ moves. That’s why heat labels exist. Pile in with everyone else and you’re often feeding the drop.

### Why did my stock dump right after I bought?
Classic pattern: you weren’t early — the crowd was already loud, heat was high, and the next tick paid that out. The labels were trying to tell you.

### Why is an “Ignored” stock going up?
Because nobody owns it. The market rewards neglect for a while. Buying in is what ends that chapter.

### Can I day-trade every few minutes?
No. Prices move on a slower schedule. This is not a tick-by-tick forex terminal, thats virtually not possible unless we overload the server.

### Is this a money printer?
No. Fees, spread, crowd behaviour, and bad luck all exist on purpose. You *can* win. You can also donate your bank to Merryweather shareholders you’ll never meet.

### Where does the money come from / go?
Trades settle from your **bank**. Buys spend bank; sells put money back in (minus fees). Your positions live with your character.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Ticker** | Short code for a company (e.g. `MER`, `ECL`, `AMU`). |
| **Share** | One unit of ownership in that company. |
| **Ask** | Buy price. |
| **Bid** | Sell price. |
| **Spread** | Gap between ask and bid. |
| **Brokerage / commission** | Fee on the trade. |
| **Bullish** | Market leaning up. |
| **Bearish** | Market leaning down. |
| **Flat** (mood) | No strong market lean. |
| **Overbought / Heating up** | Crowd interest is high — danger zone. |
| **Ignored** | Unowned; tends to drift up until someone buys. |
| **Held** | Someone owns it; quiet climb is off. |
| **Selling off** | Crowd dumping. |
| **Tick / next move** | The scheduled moment when prices actually update. |
| **Portfolio** | Everything you currently hold. |

## Bottom line

BALLSAQ is LS’s stock market: half gossip, half bloodsport, half “why did eCola just do that.”

Read the **heat**, respect the **fees**, don’t trust the **crowd**, or player actions and remember you’re trading a **game city** — messy, dramatic, and absolutely capable of ruining your afternoon for sport.

Good luck. Don’t say we didn’t warn you.
