# Trucking

Buy a truck, take jobs from the Trucking Center, and haul cargo around the map. Longer routes and better trucks unlock as you rank up. Pay hits your **bank** when you turn the job in at the depot — not at each stop.

## How to start

1. Buy your first truck at **Dock Tees Truck Sales**. Start with a **Minivan**.
2. Store it in your **Truck Depot** (same dealership flow).
3. Go to the **Trucking Center** at the docks, press **E**, and pick a route you're allowed to run.
4. Take the truck from the depot, load if the job asks for it, and follow GPS.

<Callout type="info">
The truck has to be **yours** and in the depot. A random van off the street won't start a route.
</Callout>

## The run

Most jobs are **several stops**. Local packages: park, grab boxes from the trunk, walk them to the door. Bigger hauls: drive to the pin and drop.

There's a clock. Faster legs can put time back on it. Blow the timer and you go back to the Trucking Center with nothing for that route.

When the last stop is done, **return to the Trucking Center** to get paid. Money from the run sits until you turn in.

<Callout type="warning">
**Cargo damage is real.** Crashes and scrapes ding the load even if you repair the truck afterward. Wrecked cargo means a smaller check.
</Callout>

Fuel burns on the road. If you pull a truck out of the depot under **20%**, expect to pay for a full tank from your **bank**.

## Leveling

Trucking goes to **level 40**. Each level bumps pay a little and can unlock bigger trucks and harder jobs.

You also get **skill points** to spend in trees — 1 point per rank from 2 through 39, then **2 points** at 40. See [Skills](#skills) below. You don't need a perfect tree to start — just haul.
A few rank gates worth knowing:

- **Level 10** — ADR certification (hazardous loads, +10% hazmat pay).
- **Level 20** — special cargo routes (Luxury Goods).
- **Level 30** — company expansion (hire AI drivers).
- **Level 40** — Master Trucker (+10% on every job, unlocks Classified Transport).

Weekly challenges reset on **Monday**. They pay **trucking XP**, not extra cash:

- Finish 5 deliveries — **10 XP**
- Bank **$500,000** from deliveries — **15 XP**
- Finish 3 specialist runs (Specialized Equipment, Luxury Goods, or Classified Transport) — **20 XP**

### Rough pay

Pay scales with the job, distance, your level, and how clean the cargo is. You get at least a few thousand even on a short one. Starter **Local Package** runs are the small end. Highway, hazmat, and late-game specialist jobs are the big end.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Early local / commercial | Thousands to low tens of thousands per route, depending on stops |
| Mid-level freight | Tens of thousands |
| Late highway / specialist | Can climb a lot higher if you keep the cargo intact |
| Turn-in | Paid to **bank** at the Trucking Center |

Don't expect the wiki number to match the receipt. Damage, time, and skills all move it.

## Company drivers

At **level 30** you can hire AI drivers for passive income. More slots unlock as you keep ranking, up to five at level 40. They pay out on their own clock — you still want to be hauling yourself for the real money.

## Tips

- Drive like the cargo is watching. It is.
- Match the truck to the job. The menu won't let you start a haul you don't have a legal truck for.
- Turn in at the depot. Leaving the last drop doesn't pay you.
- Fuel up. Running dry mid-route is a wasted clock.
- Spend skill points when you have them, but don't wait on a "perfect" build to take jobs.
- Job Knowledge is for scouting. Rank up before you try to actually take the fancy routes.
