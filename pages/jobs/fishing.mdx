# Fishing

Cast anywhere there's real water, land a catch, and sell it at a fish market. No clock-in, no wanted level — just a rod and some patience.

## How to start

1. Buy a **fishing rod** at any [hardware store](/features/shops) for **$100,000**.
2. Go to water.
3. Use the rod from your inventory, or type `/fish`.

You keep the rod. It isn't used up when you cast.

## Where you can fish

Stand on the bank **facing the water**, or actually in it — wading, on a jetty, or on a boat.

Any real body of water works. If you're looking at dry land, or you're too far from the water, the cast won't go.

| Water | What you'll see |
| --- | --- |
| Coast | Everyday saltwater fish. Moonfish at night. |
| Open ocean | Tuna, marlin, sharks — and the biggest legendaries. |
| Port / canals | Dirty water. More junk, weird polluted fish, night legendaries. |
| Alamo Sea | Inland salt. Leviathan only bites in the rain. |
| Lakes / reservoirs | Bass, trout, pike. Golden Trout in daylight. |
| Rivers / creeks | Trout and salmon. Ghost Salmon at night. |
| Ponds | Small fish and koi. Emerald Koi is the pond legendary. |

<Callout type="info">
**Cancel a cast** with **X** if you want the line back before something bites.
</Callout>

## Catching a fish

1. Cast and wait **15–30 seconds**. Don't wander off.
2. When the bite hits, press **E** to set the hook. You've got about two seconds. Miss it and the fish gets away.
3. Keep the green bar on the fish. **Hold Space** (or hold left click) to lift the bar; let go to drop it.
4. Fill the catch meter before the fish wears you out.

Bigger, rarer fish fight harder. Junk still counts as a catch — you can sell that too.

## Selling your catch

Look for **Fish Market** blips. There are buyers at:

- Del Perro Pier
- Paleto Bay
- Grapeseed

Walk up and press **E** to sell everything you're carrying. Paid in **cash**.

`/fishprices` shows what the market would pay for the fish in your inventory right now. Prices shift each server restart — use that command for the exact number.

### What you can catch

Rarity is about how often it bites, not a separate inventory. A legendary is roughly **one in five hundred** casts in the water it lives in — and only if the time or weather is right.

**Night** is 8 PM–6 AM. **Day** is 6 AM–8 PM. Rain means actual rain or a thunderstorm.

### Rough sale prices

Ballpark before the market modifier. A good restart pays a bit more; a bad one a bit less.

| Catch | Typical sale |
| --- | --- |
| Junk | Tens to a few hundred dollars. A waterlogged phone is the odd one out — around a grand. |
| Common fish | About **$2,000–$12,000** |
| Uncommon fish | About **$16,000–$42,000** |
| Rare fish | About **$48,000–$200,000** |
| Legendary fish | About **$480,000–$1.4 million** |

Treasure chests are a separate bonus: extra cash plus a trinket, not a fish sale.

## Tips

- **The water matters.** Coast, open ocean, lakes and rivers, and dirty water (port, canals) all pull different fish. Move around if you want variety.
- **Time and weather.** Night-only species won't bite in daylight. Catfish and the Alamo Leviathan only bite in the rain. Golden Trout wants daytime.
- **Clean catch.** If you never let the fish leave the bar, you get extra XP and a **1 in 4** chance at a second fish of the same kind (not junk).
- **Chests.** About **1 in 10** non-junk fights spawn a treasure chest. Keep the bar on it long enough to crack it open for **$2,000–$12,000** plus a trinket (pearls, coins, the occasional gold bar).
- **Legendaries.** Landing one is announced in chat. They're rare on purpose — fish the right water at the right time.
