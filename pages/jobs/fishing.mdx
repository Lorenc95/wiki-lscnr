# Fishing

Cast anywhere there's real water, land a catch, and sell it at a fish market. No clock-in, no wanted level — just a rod and some patience.

## How to start

1. Buy a **fishing rod** at any [hardware store](/features/shops).
2. Go to water.
3. Use the rod from your inventory, or type `/fish`.

You keep the rod. It isn't used up when you cast.

## Where you can fish

Stand on the bank **facing the water**, or actually in it — wading, on a jetty, or on a boat.

Any real body of water works: coast, open ocean, lakes, rivers, creeks, ponds, the port, even storm channels. If you're looking at dry land, or you're too far from the water, the cast won't go.

<Callout type="info">
**Cancel a cast** with Backspace if you want the line back before something bites.
</Callout>

## Catching a fish

1. Cast and wait. Don't wander off.
2. When the bite hits, press **E** to set the hook. Miss it and the fish gets away.
3. Keep the green bar on the fish. **Hold Space** (or hold left click) to lift the bar; let go to drop it.
4. Fill the catch meter before the fish wears you out.

Bigger, rarer fish fight harder. Junk still counts as a catch — you can sell that too.

## Selling your catch

Look for **Fish Market** blips. There are buyers at:

- Del Perro Pier
- Paleto Bay
- Grapeseed

Walk up and press **E** to sell everything you're carrying.

`/fishprices` shows what the market would pay for the fish in your inventory right now. Prices shift each server restart — use that command for the exact number.

### Rough sale prices

Ballpark before the market modifier. A good restart pays a bit more; a bad one a bit less.

| Catch | Typical sale |
| --- | --- |
| Junk (boots, cans, seaweed) | Tens to a few hundred dollars. A waterlogged phone is the odd one out — around a grand. |
| Common fish | About **$3,000–$12,000** |
| Uncommon fish | About **$15,000–$40,000** |
| Rare fish | About **$40,000–$200,000** |
| Legendary fish | About **$400,000–$1.4 million** |

Treasure chests are a separate bonus: extra cash plus a trinket, not a fish sale.

## Tips

- **The water matters.** Coast, open ocean, lakes and rivers, and dirty water (port, canals) all pull different fish. Move around if you want variety.
- **Time and weather.** Some species only bite at night. Some only bite in the rain.
- **Clean catch.** If you never let the fish leave the bar, you can get extra XP and sometimes a second fish of the same kind.
- **Chests.** A treasure chest can show up during the fight. Keep the bar on it long enough to crack it open for bonus cash and a trinket.
- **Legendaries.** Landing one is announced in chat. They're rare on purpose.
